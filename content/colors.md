# Kotopes Color Palette

Based on Instagram aesthetic for a pet hotel/care facility, this palette is warm, welcoming, and trustworthy.

## Primary Colors
- **Warm Orange**: #FF8C42 — Energy, warmth, friendliness (primary CTA)
- **Teal Accent**: #2A9D8F — Trust, calm, balance (secondary accents)
- **Warm Cream**: #FFF8F0 — Background, safe/cozy feel

## Secondary Colors
- **Deep Brown**: #5C4033 — Earth, reliability (text/headers)
- **Light Gray**: #F5F5F5 — Clean, neutral background
- **Soft Gold**: #FFD700 — Accent, highlights

## Neutral/Text
- **Dark Charcoal**: #2B2B2B — Primary text
- **Medium Gray**: #666666 — Secondary text
- **Light Border**: #E0E0E0 — Dividers, subtle elements

## Usage Guidelines
- **CTA Buttons**: Warm Orange (#FF8C42) on white or cream backgrounds
- **Headers**: Deep Brown or Dark Charcoal
- **Cards**: White or Cream background with teal accents
- **Links/Hover**: Teal accent
- **Success/Positive**: Warm Orange
- **Backgrounds**: Light Gray or Warm Cream
